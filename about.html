About

A small,
senior practice.

Alturatech is a small, senior team. No pyramid, no bench — the two founders in the room, plus a dedicated engineering team available when the roadmap needs to become software.

💡

Why we started this.

We met while working together at Microsoft. Since then, we've each built, scaled, and advised businesses independently — inside big tech, inside startups, and as embedded advisors.

After a year both spent heads-down in operating roles, Alturatech reunites that combined experience into a single boutique practice. It's the place we've always wanted to run: small, senior, and hands-on.

🎯

What we believe.

Most AI and growth work fails because it stops at the slide deck. The right agentic framework doesn't matter if no one ships it. A positioning workshop doesn't help if the pricing model can't hold it up.

So we work hands-on with founders and leadership — and when the roadmap needs to become software, our engineering team ships it with you.

How we work

Diagnose → Roadmap → Ship.

1

Diagnose

A short, focused engagement to understand the business, the team, and the product. We stress-test the problem you think you have before proposing what to do about it.

2

Roadmap

A concrete plan — sequenced, with owners and metrics — for how AI, product, GTM, or growth work will move the numbers you care about.

3

Ship

Ongoing operating support, plus engineering execution when needed. We stay in the room until the plan is running in production.
