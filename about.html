About Alturatech

Helping founders turn AI ambition into shipped product — not just strategy decks.

Alturatech is founder-led, not agency-run — every engagement gets senior judgment, not a rotating bench of associates. Product, AI, GTM, and growth are treated as one connected problem, not separate workstreams handed to separate teams. And when strategy needs to become software, our own engineering team builds it — no handoff, no black box.

💡

Why we started this

Across a decade of engagements, we kept hitting the same wall: businesses wanted to adopt AI but couldn't tie it to an actual problem worth solving, couldn't justify the cost against a clear return, and stalled the moment it was time to scale or take it to market. That gap — between AI ambition and AI outcomes — is why Alturatech exists.

🎯

What we believe

Most AI and growth work fails at the handoff — a recommendation nobody implements. The right agentic framework doesn't matter if no one ships it. A positioning workshop doesn't help if the pricing model can't hold it up. So we work hands-on with founders — and when the roadmap needs to become software, we bring in engineering support to ship it with you.

How we work

Diagnose → Roadmap → Ship

1

Diagnose

A short, focused engagement to understand the business, the team, and the product — before we quote a plan.

2

Roadmap

A written roadmap, sequenced with owners and metrics, for how AI, product, GTM, or growth work will move the numbers you care about.

3

Ship

Ongoing operating support, plus engineering execution when needed — from MVP builds to platform modernization, until the plan is running in production.
