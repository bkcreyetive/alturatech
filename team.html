Founders.

Co-Founder
BK

BK Narayanan

Co-Founder — Product & AI Strategy
AI Product Strategy B2B SaaS Platform & Growth 0-to-1 Product

19+ years of experience spanning consulting, hardware supply chain, and B2B SaaS product leadership. Most recently, BK was Head of Product at Zenoti, driving revenue growth in health & wellness through AI agents that convert, nurture, and retain customers.

He spent over a decade at Microsoft — starting as Senior Program Manager launching the first-ever Surface RT and scaling Xbox distribution, then leading the rebuild of Microsoft's global reverse-logistics supply chain onto Azure (saving $3M annually) and a $5M Amazon marketplace integration for Surface and Xbox in APAC, and finally as Principal Product Manager in the Office Product Group building AI-powered Copilot creation workflows behind Microsoft Create and OneDrive. Along the way, he incubated Microsoft Tutoring Services from scratch and led a freelancer-discovery integration with Upwork inside Teams.

BK began his career at Deloitte Consulting leading enterprise SAP transformation programs, and is also an angel investor. Since relocating back to India after 15 years in the U.S., he mentors product managers, leaders, and startup founders through ADPList. He holds an MBA from the University of Washington's Michael G. Foster School of Business.

Co-Founder
RK

Ria Kapila

Co-Founder — AI, GTM & Growth
AI & Agentic Strategy GTM Startup Advising Product Strategy

Chief AI & Product Officer at Evergent Technologies, where she leads the evolution of a subscription-billing platform into an AI-native, autonomous revenue operating system. She is also Founder & Managing Partner at Factorial Advisors, working as an embedded strategic and operating advisor to venture-backed and PE-backed startups across Generative AI, SaaS, and Consumer Tech — driving product-market fit, GTM pivots, and CX-led growth.

Ria was previously Head of Product at Magnify (backed by Madrona Ventures and Decibel Partners), and held leadership roles at Microsoft and Amazon across Devices & Software, Enterprise Cloud, SaaS, AI/ML, and low-code/no-code business applications. She has also co-founded Ki, an AI relationship operating system, and served as an Entrepreneur in Residence at Antler and a startup mentor at T-Hub.

She was recognized as a 40 Under 40 business leader in the Greater Seattle region, is an adjunct professor at the University of Washington, a Techstars mentor, and an active angel investor across AI, SaaS, and consumer startups. Ria holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a Gold Medal in Bachelor's of Technology from JNTU.
